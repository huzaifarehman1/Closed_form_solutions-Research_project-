# Closed_form_solutions-Research_project-

This is a large research project focused on finding **closed-form and partial closed-form solutions for neural networks**, with the ultimate goal of eliminating or substantially reducing the conventional training phase.

The central idea is to investigate whether neural-network parameters can be obtained analytically through mathematical optimization rather than iterative methods such as gradient descent. The long-term objective is to determine whether analytically solved neural networks can compete with, or potentially outperform, conventionally trained models while reducing training time and computational cost.

## Research Structure

The project follows a structured, incremental research process. Each experiment, derivation, result, and conclusion is stored as a separate file.

Files follow the naming convention:

```text
(part_number).(subpart_number/optional).(name)
```

For example:

```text
1.2.Defining_problem
```

The numbering reflects the logical progression of the research, while subparts are used when a section contains multiple related experiments or investigations.

The project will contain:

* Mathematical formulations of the neural-network optimization problem
* Derivations of closed-form and partial closed-form solutions
* Experiments with different architectures and activation functions
* Greedy and layer-wise analytical approaches
* Comparisons against conventionally trained neural networks
* Analysis of failure cases and limitations
* Experimental results and visualizations
* Conclusions and directions for future research

The overarching research question is:

> **Can the training of neural networks be replaced, entirely or partially, by analytical solutions?**
